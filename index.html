Inside my little corner ♡

Kono Corner is a small creative space made for people who love collecting, decorating and making their favourite little things feel more personal — one sticker, one photocard, one binder page at a time.

It started as a hobby of decorating my own binders between comebacks, and slowly turned into a corner where I get to make that same cosy, "this is so me" feeling for other collectors too. Every deco, design and preloved item is picked or made with actual care — never mass-produced, never rushed.

If you're into photocards, stationery, cute clutter and turning ordinary things into something that feels like yours — welcome in, make yourself at home.
